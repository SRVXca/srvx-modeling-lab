# SRVX — Manufacturer enquiry guide (not yet sent)

**Purpose:** ask actual OEMs for options to supply small initial volumes of cold-climate air-source heat pumps to Québec, with independent properly licensed installation. Explore two models separately: manufacturer-branded direct purchase and optional private-label/ODM if actually offered.

## First email (English)

**Subject:** SRVX Canada — direct manufacturer supply enquiry for cold-climate heat pumps

Hello,

I am developing SRVX, a Québec-based platform intended to supply residential heat pumps directly to customers, with installation performed by independent appropriately licensed local contractors.

We are researching direct manufacturer relationships rather than committing to a distributor or white-label model in advance. Could you connect me with your export/OEM or Canadian business-development team?

I would appreciate an initial discussion of:

1. Whether you can supply manufacturer-branded residential cold-climate heat pumps to a new Canadian commercial buyer, and whether OEM/ODM/private label is an available **separate** option;
2. Trial order minimums, MOQ by model, indicative ex-works/FOB and landed-Canada cost structure, payment and production lead times, Incoterms, Canadian distribution restrictions;
3. Exact AHRI-rated outdoor/indoor/furnace combinations, ENERGY STAR/NEEP/manufacturer technical submissions, safety/efficiency approvals for Canada and any documented Québec incentive-list relevance;
4. Manufacturer-held warranty options, parts and repair coverage, Canadian service support, and responsibilities for applicable Québec consumer warranties;
5. Engineering materials: dimensioned drawings/STEP/CAD or 3D objects and capacity/input-power/COP performance tables at 47°F, 17°F, 5°F or lower, including minimum/rated/maximum test operating levels;
6. Optional connectivity, device telemetry/API availability, consumer consent/privacy expectations and remote diagnostic capabilities.

At this stage we are requesting capabilities and preliminary terms, not placing an order or proposing exclusivity. Please let me know the appropriate contact and your initial requirements.

Thank you,
SRVX — Québec, Canada

## First response evaluation

| Gate | What counts as evidence? | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual OEM | Full registered entity, factory address, product-specific origin, company/group control or production authorization | Do not accept 'we are a factory' as sole proof |
| Canadian sellability | Matching model-pair certificates, applicable safety/efficiency registration and trademark distribution authorization | Do not confuse a US AHRI result with permission to import/sell in Québec |
| Supply economics | Written price + currency + MOQ + Incoterm + freight + duty/tax + warranty reserve + parts | Compare landed cost, not just headline FOB |
| Warranty | Written manufacturer obligation, duration, transferability, local service/parts and Quebec legal exposure | Manufacturer promise alone cannot erase seller obligations |
| Performance | Manufacturer data provenance including temperature, speed, cap, power, COP, ratings, exact matching SKU | Missing details remain missing; no interpolation posed as measured |
| 3D | Product CAD, dimensioned exterior drawings, photograph/render usage rights | No generic AC image passed off as exact heat pump model |
| Connectivity | Supported controller/gateway and API, access license, ownership, customer authorization and security | No assumed telemetry capabilities |
